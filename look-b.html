    The Session

    What This Program Addresses

    The Problem

    Law is adversarial by design, and the pressure lands on the lawyer. Reactivity in a deposition, a hostile email from opposing counsel, or a client who blames you for the outcome distorts judgment, strains client relationships, and erodes civility and credibility. Most conflict training teaches communication scripts. This program goes earlier in the chain — to the perception that drives the reaction.

    The Solution

    A structured, repeatable method for maintaining professional judgment under pressure, taught by a practicing attorney and a Demartini Method facilitator. A live volunteer demonstration lets attorneys see the method work on a real conflict in real time — not described, but seen.

    Monday Morning Behaviors

    What Attorneys Handle Differently

    Identify Triggers

    Why certain opposing counsel, clients and judges get under your skin — and trace the charge to its source

    Apply In Real-Time

    A self-governance process mid-conversation — in depositions, negotiations and hearings

    Reframe Conflict

    As information rather than threat — so strategy, not reaction, drives the next move

    Communicate Precisely

    With clients and opposing counsel under pressure, reducing reactive escalation and the emails you regret

    Practice With Civility

    Even when the other side doesn’t — without suppressing or performing

    Return Independently

    To the process on their own — they leave with method, not just feeling

    Let the argument go. Get back to work.
